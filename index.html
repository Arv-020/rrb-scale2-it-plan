How to crack it

Six sections, each with its own timer, unequal marks, −¼ for a wrong answer. Put your effort where the marks per hour are highest:

#SectionQs · MarksTimeHow to win it
1Financial Awareness40 · 4015 minThe cheapest 40 marks: pure recall, about 22 seconds a question. Banking static + Govt Schemes + Jul–Nov banking/RBI news. Cards every Saturday. Target 28+.
2Professional Knowledge (IT)40 · 4030 minYour home ground. All 40 classes at 2×; each class's Quiz + Imp Qs is your PK question bank. Networks, DBMS/SQL, OS and DS/Algo carry the most. Target 26+.
3Reasoning40 · 4030 minOne class every night; puzzles + seating are about half the paper. In the exam: inequality, syllogism and coding first, then puzzles. Target 25+.
4Quant + DI40 · 4030 min7–8 AM daily, and practised again at 8:30 PM. Arithmetic, then every DI type. Simplification/approximation are quick marks once calculation is fast. Target 24+.
5Computer Knowledge40 · 2015 minHalf a mark each, and about half overlaps PK. Notes for the basics; watch only what's unique. Target 13+.
6English40 · 2030 minHalf a mark each. Watch the exam-type classes; grammar from Notes + Imp Qs. Target 12+.

Pattern as reported by published guides for 2026 (240 Qs, 200 marks, 150 min); check it against your notification. Targets add up to about 128/200 and are suggested working goals, not official cut-offs.

One class, step by step (from basics)

1 · Watch actively

  • Speed as shown. Lost on something new? Drop to 1.25×; weekends have room.
  • When an example starts, pause and solve it first, then play.
  • Write only formulas, shortcuts and traps. The class PDF has the rest.

2 · Practise the same day

  • Quiz, untimed. You're learning, not racing.
  • Imp Qs: aim for about 1 minute each.
  • Each wrong answer → one line in your error log: topic, question, why.

3 · Repeat it spaced out

  • Quant: practised again at 8:30 PM the same day.
  • Saturday: 45-min weekly revision of the week's error log + Cards.
  • December: Notes/PDF only, never the full video again.

4 · Then speed

  • From November the free tests train section timing (30/30/30/15/30/15).
  • Push speed only once accuracy is above 80%.
  • Before a mock, fix your section order. After it, an hour of analysis.

Missed a day?

  • It shows under "Pending" on Today. Clear it on the weekend; don't double up on weeknights.
  • More than 4 nights behind: drop CA videos first (keep the Daily Quiz), then Mains-puzzle repeats, then English. Never drop PK, quizzes, mock analysis or sleep.

Rules that win marks

  1. Accuracy over attempts. At −0.25, answer only if you can rule out at least 2 options.
  2. No borrowing time between sections. Practise each with its own timer from November.
  3. One error log, reviewed every Saturday and twice in December.
  4. Don't chase live classes (10 AM–6 PM). Recordings are ready by night; the calendar follows release dates.
